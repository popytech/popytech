## Overview

**GNAKRY SHOP** is the commerce product of the GNAKRY ecosystem, focused on helping African businesses sell products and services through modern digital storefronts and structured commerce workflows.

The implementation repository is **private**. This public brief presents the product direction without exposing proprietary code or operational infrastructure.

---

## Product direction

- Digital storefronts
- Product and service catalogues
- Order-management workflows
- Merchant operations
- Payment and communication integrations
- Commerce experiences adapted to local markets

---

## Role in GNAKRYGROUP

GNAKRY SHOP is designed to connect commerce with the wider GNAKRY stack, including payments, messaging, business operations and technical delivery.
