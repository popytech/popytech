## Overview

**GNAKRY TRUST** is the verification and trust layer of the GNAKRY ecosystem.

Its product direction is centered on helping digital businesses establish stronger trust between users, merchants, partners and transactions through structured verification and risk-control workflows.

The implementation repository is **private**. This public brief intentionally contains no proprietary implementation details.

---

## Product direction

- Identity and profile verification
- Merchant and business verification
- Trust signals and validation workflows
- Risk-control tooling
- Verification services designed to integrate with other GNAKRY products

---

## Role in GNAKRYGROUP

GNAKRY TRUST is designed to strengthen the reliability of the ecosystem by providing a reusable verification layer for payments, commerce, business services and other digital workflows.
