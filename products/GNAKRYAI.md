## Overview

**GNAKRYAI** is the artificial-intelligence product layer of the GNAKRY ecosystem, focused on practical AI use cases for African businesses.

The goal is not AI for demonstration. The product direction is centered on business workflows: assistance, automation, operational intelligence and integrations that can improve how organizations work.

The implementation repository is **private**. This public brief communicates the product direction without exposing proprietary code or infrastructure.

---

## Product direction

- Business-focused AI assistants
- Workflow automation
- Knowledge and operational intelligence
- API and SaaS integrations
- AI capabilities adapted to local business realities

---

## Role in GNAKRYGROUP

GNAKRYAI is the intelligence layer of the ecosystem, designed to connect AI capabilities with the communication, payment, management and development products built across GNAKRYGROUP.
