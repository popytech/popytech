## Overview

**GNAKRY BUSINESS** is the business-management product layer of the GNAKRY ecosystem.

Its direction is to centralize practical operational tools for companies and organizations: customer management, internal workflows, business data and day-to-day execution.

The product repository is **private**. This document is a public overview only.

---

## Product direction

- Customer and lead management
- Business workflow organization
- Operational dashboards
- Internal follow-up and activity tracking
- Modular management tools for African SMEs and organizations

---

## Role in GNAKRYGROUP

GNAKRY BUSINESS is designed as the operational layer connecting business activity with other ecosystem services such as communication, payments and digital-product infrastructure.
